The experience

An evening at Säde

Everything is built around one unhurried service — from the first pour to the last bite.

01

Seasonal tasting menu

Nine courses that change weekly. A vegetarian path with forty-eight hours notice. Allergies noted at booking.

02

The room

Low light, linen napkins, and an open kitchen you can watch from every seat. Punavuori outside; quiet inside.

03

Wine & ferments

Nordic bottles curated by our sommelier, plus house ferments — birch sap, sea buckthorn, last summer’s berries.

04

Private sittings

Buy out the room on Sunday evenings. Birthdays, small teams, or a table that wants the kitchen to itself.
