FAQ

Questions before you book

Do you take new patients?+

Yes. Tell us if you are nervous or need a translator — we adjust the visit length.

Can I book same-day?+

We reserve slots for urgent cases. Call or use the appointment form and select “Emergency”.

Do you help with insurance?+

We prepare invoices and codes so claims are straightforward. Coverage depends on your plan.

What if I have dental anxiety?+

Ask for an anxiety visit. Longer appointments, breaks on request, and no pressure to decide on the spot.

Ready for a calmer dental visit?

Request a time online or call us — no pressure to book treatment on the first appointment.
